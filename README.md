چرا این برنامه ساخته شد؟
هر روز یک سؤال تکراری در هر خانه‌ای پرسیده می‌شود: «امروز چی بپزم؟»
پیدا کردن ایده، فکر کردن به مواد لازم و حواس‌جمعی به سلامت غذا وقت و انرژی زیادی می‌گیرد. «امروز چی بپزم؟» ساخته شد تا جواب این سؤال را سریع، ساده و سالم بدهد:

Why was this app built?
Every day, in almost every home, the same question comes up: “What should I cook today?”
Finding ideas, thinking about ingredients and keeping meals healthy takes time and energy. What to Cook Today? was built to answer that question quickly, simply and healthily:
Daily meal ideas without long searching
Clear step-by-step recipes
Calories and macros per serving for more informed choices
Persian-first, with a modern, phone-friendly design
No sign-up, no ads, no personal data collection
Features
Recipes and foods
Thousands of healthy recipes with per-serving calories, protein, carbs and fat
Preparation time and difficulty level for each recipe
Adjustable servings with automatic ingredient scaling
A foods table for looking up nutrition data
Search, categories and sorting (for example, quickest first)
“Recently viewed” and a daily featured pick
Personalization
Save your favorite recipes
Add your own photo to any recipe (stored on your device)
Create a file with your photos embedded so they stay when you share it
Backup and restore of saved items
Persian and English with one tap
Light, dark and automatic themes
Look and feel
Modern glass-style design with soft orange tones
Floating bottom tab bar and smooth animations
Mobile-first with full right-to-left (RTL) support
Technical
Installable on Android and iPhone (PWA)
Works offline after the first load
Lightweight, no server required
Your data stays on your device
Privacy and terms
The app has no accounts or ads and does not collect personal data. The full Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Copyright notice and Nutrition Disclaimer are available at the bottom of the app’s Profile screen.
Nutrition disclaimer
Calories and macros are estimates and do not replace advice from a doctor or dietitian. Always check ingredients for allergies yourself.
